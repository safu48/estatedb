Review Ownership Security

The underlying DELETE checks both the review ID and your own session user ID: 

DELETE FROM reviews
 WHERE review_id  = :rid
   AND customer_id = :cid

Ownership validation directly inside the WHERE clause prevents unauthorized tampering.
