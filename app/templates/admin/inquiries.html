Deliberately Read-Only for Administrators

Administrative oversight

Administrators can monitor every lead across the platform but do not reply directly. Lead communications belong to the assigned agent's desk to maintain clear accountability.

The primary purpose of this screen is pipeline oversight: identifying dormant 'NEW' inquiries and balancing agent workloads.

Enforced by CHECK (status IN ('NEW','CONTACTED','SCHEDULED','CLOSED')) and stored procedure sp_update_inquiry_status.
